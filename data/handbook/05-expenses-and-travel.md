# Expenses and Travel

## Principle
Spend the company's money as carefully as you would spend your own, and never
hide a decision you would be uncomfortable explaining.

## Day-to-day expenses
Submit in Expensify with a receipt. Under EUR 100 needs no pre-approval; your
manager reviews after the fact. Over EUR 100 needs manager approval first.

Reimbursement goes out with payroll, on the **25th** of each month. Anything
submitted after the 18th lands the following month.

## Travel
Book through TravelPerk. Economy for flights under six hours, premium economy
above. Trains instead of flights where the journey is under five hours.

Hotels up to **EUR 180 a night** in most cities, **EUR 260** in London, Zurich,
New York and San Francisco. Beyond that, ask first.

Per diem for meals is **EUR 55 a day**, paid automatically, no receipts.

## Team offsites
Two company-wide offsites a year, fully covered. Team-level offsites need
budget sign-off from the department lead.

## Learning budget
**EUR 1,200 a year** for courses, books and conferences, plus up to five
working days. Conference travel comes out of the learning budget, not the
travel budget. Unused budget does not carry over.

## What we do not reimburse
Fines and penalties, alcohol beyond a shared team meal, upgrades paid for
personal comfort, and family travel.
