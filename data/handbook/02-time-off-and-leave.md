# Time Off and Leave

## Annual leave
**30 days a year**, excluding public holidays in your country of residence.
Up to 10 unused days carry into the next year and must be used by 31 March.

Book in Humaans. Anything over five consecutive days needs two weeks notice so
the team can cover. Your manager approves; there is no second approver.

## Public holidays
You take the public holidays of your country of residence. If you want to work
a local holiday and take a different day instead, tell your manager — no
paperwork.

## Sick leave
Tell your manager as early as you can and mark it in Humaans. No doctor's note
is needed for the first **five consecutive days**. Beyond that, local law
applies and People will tell you what is required. Sick days are not capped
and do not come out of annual leave.

## Parental leave
Birthing parents get **20 weeks at full pay**. Non-birthing parents and
adoptive parents get **12 weeks at full pay**, which can be split into up to
three blocks within the first 18 months.

This sits on top of any statutory entitlement in your country; where the
statutory minimum is more generous, you get the statutory amount.

### Process
1. Tell your manager and People when you are ready — we ask for **eight weeks
   notice** where possible, but nobody is penalised for less.
2. People confirm the dates in writing and arrange cover with your manager.
3. A handover document is expected in the last two weeks.
4. Returning is phased: the first two weeks back are at 60% hours, full pay.

Questions go to Dilara Yilmaz in People.

## Unpaid leave
Up to three months of unpaid leave is possible after two years with the
company, at your manager's and People's discretion.
