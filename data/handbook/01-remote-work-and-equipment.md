# Remote Work and Equipment

Northbeam is remote-first. There is no required office attendance and no core
location. We do ask for four hours of overlap with 10:00–18:00 CET.

## Equipment budget
Every employee gets **EUR 2,000 in their first year** for a home setup, and
**EUR 800 per year after that**, which does not roll over. Contractors on
engagements longer than six months get the recurring EUR 800 but not the
first-year uplift.

The budget covers laptop peripherals, desk, chair, monitor, lighting,
headphones and internet upgrades. It does not cover the primary laptop, which
is issued by IT, and it does not cover phones.

### Claiming
1. Buy the item yourself.
2. Submit the receipt in Expensify under the category **Home Office** within
   60 days of purchase.
3. Your manager approves. Anything above EUR 600 in a single item also needs
   Finance approval, which Mirela Petrov handles.
4. Reimbursement lands with the next payroll run.

Items over EUR 400 remain company property and go back to IT when you leave.
Below that threshold they are yours.

## Co-working
If you would rather not work from home, we reimburse up to **EUR 250 a month**
for a co-working desk. Submit it monthly under **Co-working**; no pre-approval
needed.

## Internet and utilities
We do not reimburse ongoing internet or electricity bills. A one-off router or
mesh upgrade is eligible under the equipment budget.
